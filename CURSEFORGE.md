**FlareUI RP** brings [Total RP 3](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/total-rp-3) names and colours to [FlareUI](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/flareui) in **WoW: Forever**, and gives TRP3’s bars a matching appearance.

- **Unit frames and player nameplates:** Show RP names on player, target, focus, target-of-target, target-of-focus, party and raid frames, and on floating player nameplates. Each label follows its own First, Last or Full Name setting.
- **Profile colours:** Enable **Look > Class Colours** on a unit frame, or choose **Class & Reaction** for nameplate health bars. Health warnings, gradients and group name colours keep priority. RP names work even without a profile colour.
- **TRP3 bars:** The toolbar uses **Quest Tracker** styling by default. The target bar and its five **At First Glance** slots use **Frames**. Choose a different style or restore TRP3’s appearance independently for the toolbar and target bar group.

Install both linked addons, then open **Total RP 3 > Settings > FlareUI RP** to configure names, colours and bar appearance. If RP health colours aren’t showing, a hint points to the FlareUI setting to change.
